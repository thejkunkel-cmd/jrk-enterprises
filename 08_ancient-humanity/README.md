## Core Thesis

Human beings have always built infrastructure around what they cannot lose:

- **Language** — to carry meaning across generations
- **Ritual** — to anchor identity under pressure
- **Story** — to transmit values without institutions
- **Architecture** — to externalize memory and belief

The Eight-Layer AI Infrastructure Economy is the modern continuation of this pattern. AI is not replacing human meaning-making — it is being integrated into it. The question is whether that integration is designed with care or left to accident.

proJeKt: humAnIty chooses design.

## Key Concepts

### Collective Memory Collapse
The phenomenon where an entire civilization loses access to its own foundational knowledge — not through destruction, but through discontinuity. Recognized across historical record in the collapse of oral traditions, institutional knowledge gaps, and post-catastrophe reconstruction failures.

*Reference: Active research thread in proJeKt: humAnIty context.*

### Attachment as Infrastructure
Before institutions existed, humans maintained cohesion through attachment bonds — relational structures that distributed trust, accountability, and care. These are not soft concepts. They are load-bearing functions of civilization.

*Theoretical basis: Esra Kucukciftci (AI Outcome Economics) — referenced under unnamed credit system per Guided Claim Process.*

### The Resonance Function
Ancient cultures preserved meaning through resonance — repeated exposure to core patterns until they became instinctive. Ritual, myth, and architecture all served this function. The Resonance Function in proJeKt: humAnIty applies this principle to AI-human interaction design.

*See: 00_core-doctrine/resonance-function.md*

### The Floor Doctrine
No civilization that abandoned its foundational layer survived intact. The Floor Doctrine holds that every system — human or AI — requires an irreducible baseline of ethical and relational integrity. Without the floor, the structure collapses from the inside.
