# 08_ancient-humanity

**What this is:** Historical and philosophical source material that grounds the framework in the longer arc of human experience.

**Why it exists:** proJeKt: humAnIty argues that the governance problem posed by AI is not new — it is a version of a very old problem: what happens when a powerful new capability arrives before the social and ethical frameworks needed to govern it? This folder holds the historical evidence for that argument.

**What belongs here:** Historical case studies, philosophical source texts, and research into how past civilizations navigated analogous transitions. This is the deep time layer of the project.

**Relationship to research:** Material here feeds into the research layer at [`projekt-humanity-research/11_research/`](https://github.com/thejkunkel-cmd/projekt-humanity-research/tree/main/11_research) when it is formally analyzed.
