## Purpose

The Outreach folder contains the protocols, templates, and governance frameworks for how JRK Enterprises communicates proJeKt: humAnIty to external audiences, including institutional partners, potential clients, evaluators, and the public.

---

## The VELA Protocol

VELA governs all external outreach from JRK Enterprises:

- **V** — Verify: Confirm the recipient and context before outreach
- **E** — Evaluate: Apply Layer 6 framework to assess fit
- **L** — Listen: Outreach is a conversation, not a broadcast
- **A** — Archive: All outreach is logged for provenance

No outreach may occur before resonance review. No outreach may claim authority not established in PROVENANCE.md.

---

## Outreach Governance

- All outreach templates must be reviewed by MASTER before use
- Outreach may not make claims about partnerships or endorsements without written confirmation
- The unnamed credit system applies to all referenced contributors in outreach materials
- Outreach must align with the Bridge Doctrine (see 00_core-doctrine/bridge-doctrine.md)
