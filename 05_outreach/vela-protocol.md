## What VELA Is

VELA is the governing protocol for all external communication from JRK Enterprises. It is not a script. It is not a funnel. It is a discipline — a set of conditions that must be true before any outreach occurs, and a set of behaviors that must remain true throughout.

VELA operates at Layer 05 of the proJeKt: humAnIty architecture. It governs how the framework crosses from internal development into external relationship.

**V — Verify**
Confirm the recipient, their context, and their readiness before making contact. No cold broadcast. Every outreach begins with a question: does this person have a real reason to hear from us?

**E — Evaluate**
Apply the Layer 06 client framework to assess fit before engagement. Not every interested party is the right party. Evaluation protects both sides.

**L — Listen**
Outreach is a conversation, not a campaign. The first message opens a door. The second message responds to what was heard. There is no third message if the door did not open.

**A — Archive**
All outreach is logged for provenance. Who was contacted. When. What was said. What response was received. This is not bureaucracy — it is institutional memory.

## VELA Levels

VELA outreach is tiered by relationship distance and stakes. Each level has different pre-conditions and post-contact requirements.

### Level 1 — Warm Signal

**Who:** Someone you have met, spoken with, or who has expressed direct interest.
**Pre-condition:** Prior interaction logged. No resonance review required, but context check required.
**Allowed:** Personal message, direct question, invitation to conversation.
**Archive:** Log contact, date, context, response.

### Level 2 — Evaluated Introduction

**Who:** A potential institutional partner, evaluator, or organizational contact with no prior interaction.
**Pre-condition:** Layer 06 fit assessment complete. VELA E-check passed. BLUE review of message before send.
**Allowed:** Formal introduction using approved templates from `email-templates.md`. Reference to proJeKt: humAnIty permitted.
**Archive:** Full outreach log entry required. Response tracking required.

### Level 3 — High-Stakes Contact

**Who:** Media, academic institutions, government or policy bodies, AI labs, large-scale partners.
**Pre-condition:** White Channel review required before outreach. Claims verified against PROVENANCE.md. Message reviewed by at least one trusted human reviewer before send.
**Allowed:** Only after all pre-conditions are met. Institutional pitch deck or `institutional-pitch.md` may be attached.
**Archive:** Full provenance entry required in root PROVENANCE.md. Not just the outreach log.

## Resonance Review

Before any Level 2 or Level 3 outreach, the operator must complete a resonance review:

```
RESONANCE REVIEW CHECKLIST
☐ I am in BLUE state (reflective, not reactive)
☐ I know why this specific person needs to hear from us
☐ I know what I am asking for (or offering)
☐ The message makes no claim not in PROVENANCE.md
☐ The template has been reviewed and approved
☐ I am prepared for no response and that is acceptable
☐ The contact is logged before I send
```

If any item is unchecked: do not send. Return to RED for more development. Outreach from a RED state produces noise.

## Outreach Governance Rules

1. All outreach templates must be reviewed before use — see `email-templates.md`
2. No partnership or endorsement claims without written confirmation on file
3. The unnamed credit system applies to all referenced contributors in outreach materials
4. All outreach must align with the Bridge Doctrine (`00_core-doctrine/bridge-doctrine.md`)
5. Follow-up is permitted once, 5–7 business days after no response
6. A second non-response ends that outreach thread — log it and close it
7. Outreach threads are never deleted from the log, even if the contact declined

## VELA Escalation (Internal Use)

When outreach receives a response that creates pressure, urgency, or ambiguity:

- **Level 1 response:** Handle in normal BLUE synthesis. Log and respond within 48 hours.
- **Level 2 response:** Pause. Do not reply immediately. Run the resonance review checklist before responding. Log your response draft before sending.
- **Level 3 response:** Treat as a White Channel trigger. Do not respond until you have run a full White Room intake. This protects both the relationship and the institution.

Refer to `OPERATOR-RUNBOOK.md` Section 4 for full VELA escalation procedures.

## Outreach Log Format

All outreach is archived. Use this format for log entries in `07_resonance-logs/`:

```
VELA OUTREACH LOG
Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
Contact: [Name / Role / Organization]
VELA Level: [1 / 2 / 3]
Channel: [Email / LinkedIn / In-person / Other]
Purpose: [What was offered or asked]
Message Reference: [template used or free-form note]
Response: [Received / No response / Declined / Ongoing]
Response Date: [YYYY-MM-DD or PENDING]
Next Action: [Follow-up / Close / White Channel / None]
Notes: [Optional — unstructured context]
```

File: `07_resonance-logs/VELA-[YYYY-MM]/[contact-name].md` or append to a monthly VELA log.

## What VELA Is Not

- VELA is not a sales funnel. There is no conversion target.
- VELA is not a volume game. One right contact outweighs 100 wrong ones.
- VELA is not urgency-driven. If it feels urgent, that is a reason to slow down, not speed up.
- VELA is not rejection-averse. A clear no is a gift. Log it and move.
