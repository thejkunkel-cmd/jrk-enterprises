## VELA Level 1 Templates — Warm Signal

*Use when: you have met this person, spoken before, or they have expressed interest.*

---

### L1-A: Re-engagement After Meeting

**Subject:** Good to meet you — wanted to share something

```
Hi [First Name],

Great connecting [at / during] [context]. I wanted to follow up with
something that might be relevant to what you mentioned about [their topic].

I’m building a framework for how organizations think about AI relationships
rather than AI tools — the attachment before the automation. Still early,
but the architecture is taking shape.

If you’re curious, I’d be glad to share more. No agenda. Just a conversation.

— Joe Kunkel
JRK Enterprises | proJeKt: humAnIty
[contact info]
```

---

### L1-B: Response to Inbound Interest

**Subject:** Re: [their subject] — glad you reached out

```
Hi [First Name],

Thanks for reaching out. What you described resonates with what I’m working
through in proJeKt: humAnIty — specifically the gap between how AI is
deployed and how humans are actually prepared to work with it.

I’d love to hear more about what’s driving your interest. Would [day/time]
work for a 30-minute call?

— Joe Kunkel
JRK Enterprises
[contact info]
```

---

### L1-C: Follow-Up After Conversation (no next step defined)

**Subject:** Following up from our conversation

```
Hi [First Name],

Wanted to follow up after we spoke. I appreciated [specific thing they said
or asked].

I’ve been thinking about [brief reflection on connection to their context].
No pressure for anything — just wanted you to know the door is open if
you want to continue the conversation.

— Joe
```
