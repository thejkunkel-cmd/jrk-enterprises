## Purpose

This document establishes the governance framework for client engagements under proJeKt: humAnIty. It is not a legal contract — that is a separate instrument. This is the **relational and structural agreement** that defines how JRK Enterprises and a client will work together within the Eight-Layer framework.

The contract framework exists to:

1. Establish mutual expectations clearly and in writing
2. Define the scope of the engagement (which layers, which phases)
3. Protect client confidentiality and IP governance
4. Create a provenance-anchored record of the engagement terms
5. Provide a clean exit and transition path if the engagement ends

### Section 5: Confidentiality and IP Governance

**Client Confidentiality:**
- All client information shared in sessions is confidential
- No client data, names, or specifics will be used in public content without explicit written permission
- Attribution follows the unnamed credit system where applicable
- MASTER authorization (Joe Kunkel / JRK Enterprises) is required before any client reference is made public

**IP Ownership:**
- All framework documents, templates, and tools provided by JRK Enterprises remain the IP of JRK Enterprises
- Client-specific deliverables created during the engagement are jointly owned unless otherwise specified
- The Eight-Layer framework, its terminology, and its governance structure are proprietary to JRK Enterprises

**Client Data:**
- Assessments, resonance logs, and session notes are archived by JRK Enterprises
- Client may request copies of their own records at any time
- Records are not deleted; they are archived under the provenance system

---

### Section 6: Mutual Commitments

**JRK Enterprises commits to:**
- Honest, thorough assessment using the Eight-Layer framework
- Confidential handling of all client information
- Documented, provenance-anchored work product
- Transparency about what the framework can and cannot do
- Referral if the engagement is not the right fit

**Client commits to:**
- Honest participation in intake and assessment
- Timely communication and availability for sessions
- Openness to the framework process, including uncomfortable findings
- Respect for the IP governance terms
- Notification if the engagement is no longer serving its purpose

---

### Section 7: Engagement Exit and Transition

**Standard Engagement Completion:**
- Final resonance log entry completed
- PROVENANCE.md updated
- CHANGELOG entry added
- Client summary delivered
- Transition plan documented if applicable

**Early Exit:**
- Either party may exit with 14 days written notice
- All work product completed to date is delivered
- Financial terms per legal contract
- Provenance record archived regardless of exit circumstance

**Referral on Exit:**
- If client needs resources beyond JRK Enterprises’ scope, a referral is provided
- Referral is a service, not an abandonment
