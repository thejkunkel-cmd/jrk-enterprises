## Purpose

This document governs how JRK Enterprises initiates a client relationship under the proJeKt: humAnIty framework. The intake process is not administrative. It is the first layer of the Eight-Layer framework applied in a real human context — a diagnostic, a calibration, and an attachment signal all at once.

---

## Intake Philosophy

A client arrives with a presenting problem. The intake protocol is designed to:

1. Surface the **real problem** beneath the presenting problem
2. Identify which **Eight-Layer gaps** are active in their context
3. Establish **resonance baseline** — how the client thinks, communicates, and attaches
4. Create a **provenance record** from the first interaction
5. Determine **fit** — is this client ready for the framework, or do they need a bridge first?

Intake is not about selling. It is about honest assessment.

## Intake Session Structure

### Block 1: Context Establishment (10–15 min)

Purpose: Understand where the client is coming from — organizationally, emotionally, and strategically.

**Questions:**
- What are you trying to build, fix, or protect right now?
- What has already been tried? What happened?
- Who else is in the room on this decision (stakeholders, partners, leadership)?
- What does success look like in 90 days? In one year?

**Facilitator note:** Listen for the gap between the stated goal and the underlying need. That gap is where the real work lives.

---

### Block 2: Eight-Layer Diagnostic (15–20 min)

Purpose: Identify which layers of the client’s AI infrastructure are active, missing, or misaligned.

**Layer Check (rapid assessment):**

| Layer | Question | Status |
|---|---|---|
| L1: Physical Infrastructure | Do you have the hardware and connectivity your AI use requires? | Active / Gap / Unknown |
| L2: Energy Systems | Is your AI usage sustainable — cost, compute, energy? | Active / Gap / Unknown |
| L3: Compute / Processing | Do you have access to sufficient compute for your use case? | Active / Gap / Unknown |
| L4: Data Infrastructure | Is your data clean, structured, and accessible for AI input? | Active / Gap / Unknown |
| L5: AI Models | Are you using the right models for your context? | Active / Gap / Unknown |
| L6: Application Layer | Do your AI tools connect to your actual workflow? | Active / Gap / Unknown |
| L7: Human Integration | Are your people trained, willing, and supported in AI use? | Active / Gap / Unknown |
| L8: Civilizational Layer | Does your AI use align with your values, mission, and long-term vision? | Active / Gap / Unknown |

**Facilitator note:** Do not rush this block. The diagnostic is also a resonance scan — watch how the client responds to each layer. Confusion, defensiveness, or surprise are data.

---

### Block 3: Attachment Signal Assessment (10 min)

Purpose: Understand the client’s relational posture toward AI and toward JRK Enterprises.

**Questions:**
- How do you currently feel about AI — excited, anxious, skeptical, resigned?
- Have you worked with an AI consultant or coach before? What happened?
- What would make you trust this process?
- What would cause you to disengage?

**Facilitator note:** These questions are not soft. They are the most structurally important block. A client who cannot answer the trust question is not ready for deep engagement. Note the attachment signal in the resonance log.

---

### Block 4: Fit Assessment (5–10 min)

Purpose: Determine whether the client is ready for the full framework or needs a bridge engagement first.

**Fit Indicators — Ready for Full Framework:**
- Clear presenting problem with real stakes
- At least 3 Eight-Layer gaps identified
- Positive or curious attachment signal
- Decision-making authority present or accessible
- Willingness to document and track progress

**Fit Indicators — Bridge Engagement First:**
- Presenting problem is vague or entirely externally defined
- Resistance to Layer 7 or Layer 8 questions
- No prior AI exposure or strong AI anxiety
- No clear stakeholder access

**Fit Indicators — Not a Fit (At This Time):**
- Looking for a vendor, not a partner
- Unwilling to be assessed
- Expects immediate ROI without framework investment
- Attached to a solution before understanding the problem

---

### Block 5: Next Steps (5 min)

- Summarize what was heard
- Name the gaps identified
- Propose engagement path (Full Framework / Bridge / Referral)
- Confirm provenance: document who was present, what was said, and what was agreed
- Schedule follow-up or deliver assessment-template.md for async completion
