## Purpose

The White Room is a structured methodology for unfiltered human-AI exploration. It is the safe container for raw intellectual work — the space before polish, before governance, before publication.

In the white room:
- Questions are asked without agenda
- AI outputs are treated as raw material, not final answers
- Human judgment is primary
- The floor doctrine applies, but performance pressure does not

---

## White Room Principles

1. **No premature closure**: Ideas are not judged before they are fully formed
2. **All voices in, no hierarchy**: White room sessions treat all input as potentially valuable
3. **The AI is a thinking partner, not an authority**: AI output is challenged, not deferred to
4. **What happens in the white room informs, but does not determine**: White room outputs require resonance review before deployment
5. **Attribution is preserved from the beginning**: Every white room session is logged for provenance

---

## White Room Session Structure

1. **Entry**: State the question or problem without pre-loaded answers
2. **Exploration**: Use AI to surface perspectives, challenge assumptions, generate options
3. **Challenge**: Actively argue against the AI's outputs
4. **Synthesis**: Identify what survived the challenge
5. **Log**: Record the session with attribution in the resonance log
6. **Exit**: Submit synthesis for resonance review before any external use
