# 03_white-room

**What this is:** The White Architect channel — governance, trust layer operations, and doctrine-adjacent interpretation.

**Function:** The White Room is the lighthouse layer of the Station Architecture. It holds the governance backbone of the project: outreach rules, voice maps, trust checklists, and the standards that the Red and Blue channels operate under.

**What belongs here:** White Spine documents, outreach voice standards, trust layer checklists, doctrine-adjacent interpretation notes.

**What does not belong here:** Active outreach execution (that goes in `05_outreach/`), public broadcast content (that goes in the Blue channel), or raw session logs.

**Relationship to doctrine:** The White Room implements doctrine — it does not write or override it. Changes to doctrine go through the research repository and MASTER review.
