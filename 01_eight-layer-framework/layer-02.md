## Definition

The Model Selection layer encompasses the process of choosing, customizing, and maintaining the AI models that power applications. This includes:

- Foundation model selection (GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, Llama, Mistral, etc.)
- Fine-tuning and domain adaptation
- Model versioning and lifecycle management
- Open-source vs. proprietary model strategy
- Multi-model orchestration decisions

---

## Role in the Eight-Layer Economy

Model selection is the first major decision point where human values enter the stack. The choice of model determines:

- **Capability ceiling**: What the system can and cannot do
- **Bias baseline**: Models carry the biases of their training data and RLHF process
- **Cost structure**: Different models have vastly different inference costs
- **Vendor dependency**: Proprietary models create lock-in risks
- **Values alignment**: Models encode different priorities around helpfulness, safety, and honesty

---

## Human Impact Considerations (Safety Spine Application)

- Bias testing required before institutional use (per Safety Spine)
- Model selection decisions should be documented and reversible
- No model may be selected for high-stakes human evaluation without independent audit
- Model limitations must be disclosed to end users

---

## Evaluation Criteria (Layer 6 Connection)

- Benchmark performance on domain-specific tasks
- Bias and fairness audit results
- Transparency of training data and methodology
- Alignment with Safety Spine principles
- Cost-to-performance ratio across use cases
