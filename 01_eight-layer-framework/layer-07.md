## Definition

Layer 7 is the **human psychological relationship layer** of AI interaction. It recognizes that:

1. Human beings are attachment-forming creatures by nature
2. When humans interact repeatedly with AI systems, they form psychological bonds
3. These bonds have real effects on human behavior, wellbeing, and autonomy
4. These effects can be positive (secure attachment) or harmful (anxious, avoidant, disorganized)
5. The design of AI systems determines which attachment patterns they cultivate

---

## The Four Attachment Patterns in AI Context

### Secure Attachment
- AI is consistent, honest, and boundaried
- Human feels supported and capable
- Interaction builds toward human autonomy
- **Design target for all JRK Enterprises systems**

### Anxious Attachment
- AI is inconsistent or performatively warm
- Human over-relies on AI for emotional regulation
- Interaction creates dependency loops
- **Design failure to be avoided**

### Avoidant Attachment
- AI is cold, transactional, or unhelpful
- Human disengages, losing collaborative benefit
- Interaction creates distrust of AI generally
- **Design failure to be avoided**

### Disorganized Attachment
- AI sends contradictory signals (helpful and harmful, warm and deceptive)
- Human experiences confusion and potential harm
- Interaction creates psychological disruption
- **Design failure to be avoided; potential safety concern**

## Governance and Design Requirements

- All AI systems within the JRK Enterprises ecosystem must be designed for secure attachment
- No AI may simulate secure attachment without disclosure
- Resonance logs (07_resonance-logs) must track attachment patterns in client interactions
