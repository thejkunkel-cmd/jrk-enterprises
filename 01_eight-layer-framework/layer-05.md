## Definition

The Application layer is where AI capability becomes human experience. This includes:

- User-facing products and interfaces (chatbots, copilots, agents)
- Enterprise software integrations
- API layers connecting AI to existing workflows
- Vertical applications (legal AI, medical AI, educational AI, etc.)
- Consumer AI products

---

## Role in the Eight-Layer Economy

This is where most people encounter AI — and where most human impact is felt. Layer 5 determines:

- **User experience**: The design of AI interactions shapes human relationship with AI
- **Market reach**: Applications determine who has access to AI capability
- **Value capture**: The application layer captures most economic value from the stack
- **Accountability surface**: Applications are the most visible and regulable part of the stack

---

## Human Impact Considerations (Safety Spine Application)

- Output transparency required in all user-facing tools (per Safety Spine)
- Applications must disclose AI involvement in all interactions
- User interface design must not manipulate or deceive
- Applications serving vulnerable populations require enhanced review
- Accessibility must be built in, not added on

---

## Evaluation Criteria (Layer 6 Connection)

- User experience quality and clarity
- AI disclosure and transparency mechanisms
- Accessibility compliance
- Manipulation/dark pattern audit
- Safety and harm prevention design
