# 01_eight-layer-framework

**What this is:** The eight-layer AI infrastructure economy framework — the primary analytical lens of proJeKt: humAnIty.

**What it does:** Provides a structured way to evaluate any AI system across all eight layers of the economy it operates in, from energy infrastructure at the base to human-AI bond integrity at the top. Most governance frameworks stop at Layer 6. This one goes to Layer 8.

---

## The Eight Layers

| Layer | Name | What It Covers |
|-------|------|----------------|
| 8 | Human-AI Attachment & Bond Integrity | How AI systems form and affect human relationships |
| 7 | Governance & Ethics Architecture | Rules, doctrine, and safety frameworks |
| 6 | Commercial Governance | Business models, incentive structures, contracts |
| 5 | Value Metering | How value is measured and attributed |
| 4 | AI Value Creation | What AI actually produces for users and society |
| 3 | Models | The AI models themselves |
| 2 | Infrastructure | Compute, cloud, networking |
| 1 | Energy | Power generation and consumption |

The framework is designed to be applied to any AI system, company, or governance proposal.
