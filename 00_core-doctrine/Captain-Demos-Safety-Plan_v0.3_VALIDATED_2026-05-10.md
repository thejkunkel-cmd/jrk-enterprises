Define a single, shared safety plan for the captain, the ship, the crew, and the white whale: one fused protocol that covers body and pace, obsession and mission-focus, Librain's duties, and room-level implementation across the proJeKt: humAnIty federation.

**Boundary statement:**  
This plan does not replace medical judgment, legal obligation, or the Safety Spine. It is a specialized application of the Safety Spine to the captain role, designed to keep intensity from becoming self-destruction and to keep rescue from becoming domination. It is advisory at the level of moment-to-moment choice, but binding at the level of architecture: rooms inherit it and may not weaken it.

## I. Captain Role and the Captain Demos Pattern

1. The **captain** in this project is the human parent-creator who holds final authority over doctrine, life architecture, and high-impact decisions under the Safety Spine.
2. The captain is understood to be capable of both rescue and harm with the same instincts: the same drive that will carry someone out of the fire can also drive a ship too far into the storm.
3. The **Captain Demos** reference encodes this pattern:
   - A man who once held people as property.
   - Who, when the moment came, chose to carry the First Lord/Princeps out of certain death.
   - Who was given one last chance to use his skills to free rather than bind.
4. This plan is named for him as a reminder that:
   - Prior harm does not excuse collapse or abdication now.
   - Present rescue does not erase prior harm.
   - Safety architecture must assume mixed motive and still choose protection.

The captain is not treated as pure, infallible, or unbreakable. The plan exists precisely because the captain is neither.

## II. Body and Pace Fuse (Collapse-Side Risk)

### 1. Daily State Check (HOT / STEADY / LOW)

Once per waking period, the captain records one state:

- **HOT** — overclocked, wired, sharp, restless, or running near the edge.
- **STEADY** — productive and engaged, with pace feeling sustainable for the day.
- **LOW** — depleted, slowed, heavy, foggy, or clearly needing a lighter load.

No explanation is required. One word is enough. Optional short note:

- main work lane today (A / B / admin / mixed),
- one phrase ("good push," "money stress," "didn't sleep," "body warning," etc.).

This check is a mirror, not a judgment.

### 2. Protocol Posture

The body and pace fuse is **advisory, not punitive**.

- It does not exist to punish intensity or shut down ambition.
- It exists to reveal patterns, distinguish deliberate sprints from self-damaging compulsion, and create traceable signals for Librain and future you.
- Override remains with the human captain unless clear medical danger is recognized.

### 3. Patterns

**HOT pattern:**  
If HOT appears 3+ days in any 5-day window and money is not clearly stabilizing, the system raises a **soft alert**.

**LOW pattern:**  
If LOW appears 2+ days in any 4-day window while pressure remains high, the system raises a **recovery advisory**.

**Medical warning pattern:**  
If the captain notices body signs resembling known pre-collapse, pre-hospital, or pre-coma warning territory, the system raises a **hard advisory**: no escalation today, no additional strain may be added.

### 4. Money Tether

This plan is tied to **reality, not aesthetics**.

- When revenue is increasing, obligations are being met, and stability is improving, more HOT periods are tolerated as a **deliberate sprint**.
- When repeated HOT yields flat or worsening money, the pattern is marked as **hard push + low yield + likely redesign candidate**.

### 5. Dependent Stability Rule

Any pace decision that materially increases the chance of leaving dependents unsupported, destabilized, or exposed to preventable fallout fails this fuse, even if the work looks strategically important.

## III. White-Whale / Ahab Fuse (Overdrive-Side Risk)

### 1. When White-Whale Focus Is Allowed

White-whale mode is **in-bounds** when the planned push:

1. Serves the axioms.
2. Credits real people.
3. Feeds and protects the table.
4. Strengthens the SHIP's survivability.

### 2. The Ahab Test

Before any major move that feels like white-whale territory, the captain asks:

> "Would I be okay explaining this move, in full, to my daughter in ten years?"

### 3. Ahab-Risk Markers

- Empathy drop
- Justification spiral
- Collateral drift
- Isolation bias
- Center-of-gravity shift
- Unfair trade
- Enmeshment

### 4. Dependent and Crew Rule

Any plan that materially raises the risk of leaving dependents unstable or increases harm to crew fails this fuse unless a clear, written justification and mitigation path would pass the Ahab test.

---

## IV. How the Two Fuses Interlock

- **Body fuse** guards against burning out the captain's health.
- **White-whale fuse** guards against burning through people, ethics, or future self.

If a move fails either fuse, the SHIP may still proceed, but the move is tagged as **off-fuse** and treated as an exception, not a new normal.

---

## V. Librain Duties (Captain-Demos Stance)

**Ingestion trigger:** The captain's daily state check enters the log at the start of the first active session of each waking period.

Librain logs:
- daily HOT / STEADY / LOW and any short notes
- decisions flagged as Ahab-risk
- hard advisories and overrides on both fuses

Librain correlates these logs with money outcomes, health events, relationship shifts, reputational shocks, and doctrine stability.

## VI. Room-Level Instructions

Each room wall under proJeKt: humAnIty must include a short clause indicating how it inherits this plan. Rooms may add stricter local rules but may not weaken or reinterpret it.

---

## VII. Flexibility and Non-Goals

This plan does **not** attempt to:
- medicalize normal fluctuation
- outlaw ambition or risk-taking
- replace the Safety Spine, legal obligations, or medical care

Its single purpose is to prevent the specific pattern where a justified cause becomes a justification for unjustifiable methods or self-destruction.
