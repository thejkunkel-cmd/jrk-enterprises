## Executive Summary

This document applies the Eight-Layer AI Infrastructure Economy framework to compare Microsoft's AI ecosystem (primarily Copilot/Azure OpenAI) against Perplexity AI as enterprise and professional AI platforms.

The comparison reveals that both platforms excel in different layers and serve different use cases. The purpose of this evaluation is not to declare a winner but to demonstrate how the Eight-Layer framework enables informed, human-centered platform decisions.

---

| Criterion | Microsoft | Perplexity |

## Interpretation

The scores are nearly identical (3.10 vs 3.05) — which is the honest answer. These are **different tools for different purposes**:

- **Microsoft** excels in enterprise infrastructure, orchestration depth, and model variety. Choose Microsoft when you need enterprise-grade compliance, deep integration with existing Microsoft infrastructure, and agentic automation.

- **Perplexity** excels in transparency, citation integrity, and human-accessible research. Choose Perplexity when you need verifiable, sourced answers and want to build toward human autonomy rather than AI dependency.

The Eight-Layer framework reveals what simple feature comparisons miss: **Perplexity performs meaningfully better on the human-centered layers (7 and 8) that most evaluations ignore.**
