## What This Is

The Blue Station is the review and synthesis node in the TESLA Protocol rotation. When an operator is at Blue, they are processing what Red collected — reading logs, synthesizing findings, writing documents, and resting their intake capacity. Blue is the back edge of the channel.

This is not a passive position. It is a reflective one.

---

## Blue Station Responsibilities

### Post-Session Audit
- Review all session logs generated during the preceding Red rotation
- Flag anomalies, gaps, or unresolved items for follow-up
- Confirm all session files follow the naming convention and contain required provenance headers

### Synthesis Work
- Write, edit, and finalize documents based on Red Station intake
- Produce resonance log entries for completed sessions
- Cross-reference new material against existing doctrine and flag any conflicts
- This is the station where final decisions and sign-offs happen

### Resonance Logging
- Convert raw session telemetry into narrative resonance logs (07_resonance-logs/)
- Capture: what happened, what held, what drifted, what needs follow-up
- Write with accuracy — do not sanitize friction; friction is data

### Calibration & Rest
- Cognitive reset is mandatory at Blue — step away from active intake
- No new client sessions, no new AI prompts during Blue unless a Level 2+ escalation requires it
- Use this time to review the GAP-ANALYSIS.md and update status of any items closed

---

## Blue Station Rules

1. No new intake sessions are opened from Blue — only review and synthesis
2. Blue has final sign-off authority on any document or decision generated during the paired Red rotation
3. Resonance logs must be completed before the next Red rotation begins
4. If a Red-flagged anomaly was not resolved, Blue must document why and what the next step is
5. Blue does not rush back to Red — the full rotation period must be observed

---

## Rotation Trigger

After the Blue rotation period (minimum 60 minutes, target 120 minutes), the operator may return to Red:

1. Confirm all synthesis tasks are complete or formally deferred with a note
2. Confirm resonance log is written for the previous session
3. Log the rotation back to Red in the session file
4. Re-enter active intake mode
